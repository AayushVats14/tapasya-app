# 📚 Tapasya

> **Turn your study hours into your biggest competitive advantage.**

**Tapasya** is a responsive study-time tracking web app built especially for **JEE and NEET aspirants in India**. It combines focused study sessions, daily commitments, social accountability, and distraction blocking into one platform.

Whether you're studying alone or with your friends, Tapasya is designed to help you **stay consistent, track your progress, and keep your focus where it belongs.**

---

## ✨ Features

### ⏱️ Study Timer

Track your study sessions and build a record of the time you've invested in your preparation.

- Start, pause, and stop study sessions
- Track focused study time
- Designed for long-term consistency

### 🎯 Sankalp — Daily Goals

**Sankalp** means a commitment or resolve.

Set your daily study goals and make a commitment to yourself before you begin studying.

> **Aaj ka Sankalp — Aaj ki mehnat.**

Use your Sankalp to stay accountable to the targets you've set for the day.

### 🏆 Global Leaderboard

See how your study time compares with students across the Tapasya community.

Compete, stay motivated, and turn consistency into a friendly competition.

### 👥 Squads

Create or join a **Squad** with your friends and study together.

Squads allow you to:

- Study alongside your friends
- Compare your progress
- Stay accountable
- Compete within your own group

Because sometimes, knowing your friends are studying too is enough motivation to open that book.

### 🏹 Arjuna — Distraction Blocker

Meet **Arjuna**, the Tapasya browser extension built to protect your focus.

Arjuna blocks distracting websites while you're studying, helping you avoid the endless cycle of:

> _"I'll just check Instagram for five minutes."_

**Focus first. Distractions later.**

### 📱 Responsive Design

Tapasya is designed to work across different screen sizes, making it accessible on:

- 💻 Desktop
- 💻 Laptop
- 📱 Mobile
- 📟 Tablet
